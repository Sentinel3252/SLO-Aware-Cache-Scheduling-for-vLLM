# Community guidelines

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical ideas on their merits,
give actionable feedback, and welcome questions from people with different experience
levels. English and Chinese discussion are welcome.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.
These expectations apply to the repository's issues, pull requests, and other
project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or
restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. Enforcement should be
proportionate to the behavior and protect the people affected.

Report a concern privately through the repository owner's available GitHub contact
method. If none is available, open an issue requesting a private contact without
identifying affected people or posting sensitive details. No response-time commitment
is currently offered.
